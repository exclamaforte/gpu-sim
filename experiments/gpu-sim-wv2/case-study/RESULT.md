# Case study: what does the harness recommend? (2026-10-24)

World=4, NVLink, 5 steps / 2 warmup, transport off (timing is real compute; only
collectives are modelled). Generated by `tools/torch_parallel/sweep`.

| d_model | config | step_ms | coll | MB/rank | comm_sim | peak MB | local params |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 512 | DP4 | 19.3 | 149 | 272.8 | 1.77 | 1067 | 16,777,216 |
| 512 | TP2+DP2 | 159.8 | 181 | 440.5 | 2.00 | 773 | 10,757,120 |
| 1024 | DP4 | 145.9 | 149 | 948.2 | 4.95 | 2155 | 58,720,256 |
| 1024 | TP2+DP2 | 91.5 | 181 | 1082.4 | 2.51 | 1416 | 34,097,152 |

## What the harness says

At d_model=1024, TP2+DP2 is the better configuration on the two axes that matter:
**36% lower step time** (91.5 vs 145.9 ms) and **34% lower peak memory** (1416 vs
2155 MB), at the cost of **14% more collective bytes** (1082 vs 948 MB/rank). The
extra traffic is a good trade because the model is large enough that compute, not
communication, is the binding constraint.

At d_model=512 the recommendation reverses: DP4 is markedly faster. TP's extra
collectives and smaller per-rank matrices do not pay for themselves at this size.

That crossover is the useful output. It is a statement about the SOFTWARE
configuration at a fixed declared fabric, not about a real cluster.

## Caveats that must travel with this table

- `step_ms` varies run to run on this shared box (the d=512 TP2 row at 159.8 ms is
  the noisiest and most suspect -- it is slower than the larger d=1024 TP2 row,
  which is not plausible as a compute effect). Traffic counts are deterministic.
- Every collective duration is SYNTHETIC: declared link parameters, never checked
  against real multi-GPU hardware (gpu-sim-wv2 is blocked on that).
- Peak memory is only comparable within one batching convention.
- Losses are not reported: this is a cost study, and `--dp-mean`/transport were off.
